Something I want to make clear.

I don’t care if you disagree with me politically, disagree with me about Israel, Bitcoin, Christianity, or believe some conspiracy theory I think is completely wrong.

I’ll disagree with you. Sometimes strongly. That’s fine. I don’t need everyone I follow to think like me.

I also don’t particularly enjoy arguing for the sake of arguing.

And after a while, if someone’s feed is mostly racial stuff, Jewish conspiracy theories, personal attacks, or just constant contempt for people they disagree with, I may simply stop following or mute them.

That’s not because my feelings are hurt. I’m just not interested in filling my feed with it.

You are free to say whatever you want. I’m equally free to decide what I want to spend my time reading.

We don’t have to agree to have a conversation. But I don’t owe anyone my attention.
