I suppose I would get more reactions if I scolded certain individuals by name.

That is not my style, and I will not apologize for refusing to do it.

I see so much visceral hatred here now, and I refuse to be drawn into a lynch-mob mentality. Accountability is necessary. Personal degradation, threats, and collective fury are not.

“Be angry and do not sin; do not let the sun go down on your anger.”

I am angry. But my anger does not require me to hate anyone.
