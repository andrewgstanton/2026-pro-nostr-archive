One Palestinian story I think more people should know is Mosab Hassan Yousef, the “Son of Hamas.”

His father was a founding Hamas leader. Mosab eventually rejected Hamas, worked with Israel’s Shin Bet, and became a Christian.

What strikes me most about his story isn’t simply that he became pro-Israel. It’s that his experiences forced him to reconsider who he thought his enemies were — and his encounter with Jesus’ teaching to love your enemies became part of that journey.

You don’t have to agree with everything he says to find his story worth hearing
